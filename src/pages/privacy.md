Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as an anonymous unique identifier. They are sent to your browser by the websites you visit and stored on your device. You can choose to accept or refuse cookies and to be told when a cookie is being sent to your device. If you refuse cookies, some portions of the Service may not work as intended.
