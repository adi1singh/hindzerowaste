In a rapidly urbanising world, municipal solid waste (MSW) has become one of the most critical environmental challenges. Cities generate thousands of tonnes of waste every day, and traditional dumping is no longer sustainable. A zero landfill solution built around a 120 TPD (tonnes per day) MSW processing plant changes that.

The approach focuses on maximum resource recovery, minimal landfill dependency and complete processing of waste through scientific treatment technologies.

## What is a zero landfill solution?

A zero landfill solution is a comprehensive waste management system in which almost all collected municipal solid waste is processed, recycled or converted into useful outputs, leaving little or nothing for landfill. Its objectives are to:

- Eliminate dependency on landfills
- Achieve maximum material recovery
- Convert waste into reusable resources
- Reduce environmental pollution
- Support a circular economy

Instead of being dumped, waste is processed through segregation at source, mechanical sorting in MRF systems, composting or bio-methanation of organic waste, RDF production, and recycling of plastics, metals and inert materials.

## Understanding 120 TPD capacity

A 120 TPD MSW plant is designed to process 120 tonnes of municipal solid waste a day using integrated treatment technologies. In practice that means:

- 120 tonnes of waste handled daily
- A capacity suited to medium and large urban municipalities
- Continuous operation of 8–16 hours a day, depending on design
- A multi-stream processing system

## Technologies in a 120 TPD plant

A modern zero landfill plant brings together:

- Trommel screens
- Shredders
- Ballistic separators
- Magnetic separators
- Wind sifters
- Conveyor systems
- Baling presses
- Composting systems

Together they deliver high efficiency, automation and resource recovery.

## The benefits of a zero landfill MSW plant

### Environmental

- Reduces landfill overflow
- Minimises soil and groundwater contamination
- Reduces greenhouse gas emissions
- Promotes cleaner cities

### Economic

- Revenue from recyclables
- RDF sales to industry
- Compost marketing
- Jobs in the waste sector

### Social

- A cleaner urban environment
- Improved public health
- Greater awareness of segregation

## Why 120 TPD suits so many cities

A 120 TPD plant is a strategic choice because it:

- Matches the waste generation of a medium-sized city
- Is easier to control operationally
- Keeps infrastructure costs in proportion
- Can be scaled for future expansion
- Balances capital and operating expenditure

## Zero landfill and the smart city

Governments and municipalities are shifting towards smart waste management systems that eliminate landfill dependency. Smart segregation, automated processing plants and resource recovery systems together move cities towards sustainable, circular waste economies.

## In summary

A zero landfill solution combined with a 120 TPD MSW plant is more than a waste management system: it is a complete environmental transformation model. It turns waste from a burden into a resource, supports sustainable development and aligns with India’s long-term urban sustainability goals. The cities that adopt it will lead clean, green and smart urban development.
